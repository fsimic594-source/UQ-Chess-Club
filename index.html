About the Club

UQ Chess Club meets weekly on the St Lucia campus for students, staff and anyone in the wider Brisbane chess community who wants a game. No rating and no experience required. Most nights are social, with boards running from 5pm until people head home.

Through the semester we also run a club championship in rapid format, a rolling blitz ladder, and variants nights covering bughouse, atomic and Chess960. Members represent UQ in intervarsity matches against QUT, Griffith, Bond and ACU.

Equipment is provided. If it's your first time, arrive a little after 5pm and someone on the committee will pair you with a game at your level.
